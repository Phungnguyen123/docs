Chatbot & CS Team Handoff on Chatwoot

How the CS team passes a WhatsApp/Livechat conversation to BSmart and back.

Core Principles

Only two fields control the handoff. Everything else follows from them.

1

Assignee = WHO

Decides whether a human or the bot handles the conversation.

CS agent assigned — bot is off.
Agent Bot assigned — bot is on.
2

Status = Pending

For new inbound conversations.

Bot replies within business hours.
Outside hours it posts the support window or a form.
3

Status = Open

For a live case that needs cover.

Reassigned to Agent Bot, the bot replies immediately, 24/7.
Business hours no longer apply.

PART 01

Handoff on WhatsApp

Four steps: bot greets, CS takes over, bot covers, ticket closes.

Step 1: First Contact

New customer arrives

The bot acts as receptionist

When a customer sends the first message on WhatsApp, Chatwoot sets:

Assignee: Unassigned / Agent Bot
Status: Pending
The bot answers from its script during business hours. Outside hours, it tells the customer when the team is back. No CS action needed.

Step 2: CS Takes Over

Human support

Turn the bot off to advise

When the case needs an agent, set both fields:

Assignee: your own name
Status: Open
The bot stops replying entirely. Every message from now on is yours.

Step 3: Hand Back to the Bot

Agent busy or off shift

The bot covers the live case

Mid-conversation, change one field only:

Assignee: change to Agent Bot
Status: keep Open
With Open kept, the bot replies immediately and around the clock — including outside business hours.

Step 4: Close the Case

End of support

Close the ticket in Chatwoot

Once the customer's request is settled:

Action: set the status to Resolved
Assignee: any — it no longer matters
The conversation is complete and the bot skips it. Reopen is still available if the customer comes back in the same thread.

After Resolved: The Loop Restarts

Customer returns

A new conversation opens at Step 1

If the customer messages again after the case was resolved, Chatwoot creates a new conversation:

Assignee: Agent Bot
Status: Pending
The original Pending flow resumes, so the bot greets the customer again and the four steps repeat from the beginning.

Recap for the CS Desk

01

Assign before you reply

Put your name on the conversation and set it to Open, or the bot keeps answering alongside you.

02

Hand back, don't park

Leaving your shift with a case Open and assigned to you leaves the customer waiting. Reassign to Agent Bot.

03

Resolve when finished

Resolved keeps the queue clean, and the next inbound message reopens the flow at Pending automatically.

⚠️ Important note
Chatwoot v4.16.2
Unlike previous versions, the current Chatwoot (v4.16.2) requires BOTH actions to stop the Bot: you MUST Assign Agent AND change Status from Pending to Open at the same time. Doing only one will NOT turn off the Bot.
